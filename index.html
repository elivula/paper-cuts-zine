I notice things: the coat taken up by someone who didn’t love it, the man who stops dead at the top of the escalator, the film everyone calls a classic and nobody has finished. I was raised on cinema that was not meant for my age, and I have been paying attention ever since, mostly to things people would rather I didn’t. Trends are not covered here. criticism of clothes, films, rooms, and the people inside themkritikë për rrobat, filmat, dhomat dhe njerëzit brenda tyre

I write criticism the way some people take inventory: slowly, and with the lights on. The subjects are clothes, films, rooms and manners, and the question is always the same, which is whether anyone was paying attention. Usually the answer is no. Here, someone was.

This is not a lifestyle blog. Nothing is for sale, nobody is sponsored, and no one will be told to love themselves. There are essays, there are notes, and there are photographs that were not deleted for a reason.

Written for anyone, anywhere, who has ever stood in a room and silently corrected it.

Shkruaj kritikë ashtu siç disa njerëz bëjnë inventar: ngadalë dhe me dritat ndezur. Temat janë rrobat, filmat, dhomat dhe sjelljet, e pyetja është gjithmonë e njëjta: a po i kushtonte dikush vëmendje? Zakonisht përgjigjja është jo. Këtu, dikush po i kushtonte.

Ky nuk është blog për stil jetese. Asgjë nuk shitet, askush nuk sponsorizohet dhe askujt nuk do t’i thuhet ta dojë veten. Ka ese, ka shënime dhe ka fotografi që nuk u fshinë me arsye.

Shkruar për këdo, kudo, që ka qëndruar ndonjëherë në një dhomë dhe e ka korrigjuar në heshtje.
